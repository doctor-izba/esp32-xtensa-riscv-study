\section{Artifact interpretation}
Exact C6 measured-build evidence is distinguished from matched reconstructed objects. The reconstruction fixes matched source and declared policies but not compiler backend, ABI, or implementation. Static instruction counts are not dynamic counts. The accepted Mode A Rust CRC ELF is the single disclosed preservation exception; its generated-code analysis is reconstruction only.

\section{Reproduction contract}
The experimental anchor is commit \texttt{933363b342ac960c0d364b4c6379b1ac884dec9f}. C6 ran at fixed/read-back 160 MHz with DFS, sleep, Wi-Fi, BLE, and IEEE 802.15.4 disabled; flash was 4 MB DIO at 80 MHz. One warm-up preceded 100 accepted observations at each of eight frozen sizes. Raw rows retain batch count, total batch duration, normalized duration, checksum, toolchain, vector hash, board, flash, and control metadata where defined by the workload schema.

\section{Interpretation boundary}
The study supports claims about the observed board/build combinations. It does not identify a pure ISA treatment effect, population hardware effect, energy result, dynamic instruction count, CPI, or cache behavior. Hardware SHA is a complete peripheral/API path. The Rust FFT transformation is a whole generated-kernel change rather than an isolated cost of checks.
