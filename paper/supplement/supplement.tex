\section{Artifact interpretation}
Exact C6 measured-build evidence is distinguished from matched reconstructed objects. The reconstruction fixes matched source and declared policies but not compiler backend, ABI, or implementation. Static instruction counts are not dynamic counts. The accepted Mode A Rust CRC ELF is the single disclosed preservation exception; its generated-code analysis is reconstruction only. The study supports claims about the observed board/build combinations, not a pure ISA treatment, population hardware effect, energy, dynamic instruction count, CPI, or cache behavior. Hardware SHA is a complete peripheral/API path; Rust FFT is a whole generated-kernel transformation.

\section{Reproduction contract}
The experimental anchor is commit \texttt{933363b342ac960c0d364b4c6379b1ac884dec9f}. C6 ran at fixed/read-back 160 MHz with DFS, sleep, Wi-Fi, BLE, and IEEE 802.15.4 disabled; flash was 4 MB DIO at 80 MHz. One warm-up preceded 100 accepted observations at each of eight frozen sizes. Raw rows retain batch count, total batch duration, normalized duration, checksum, toolchain, vector hash, board, flash, and control metadata where defined by the workload schema.
